# Fictional dataset — Fjordhus Retail A/S

> **Everything in this file and in `src/data/seed/` is fictional.** Fjordhus Retail A/S is
> an invented company. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

Generated by `scripts/generate-seed.ts` (deterministic: `npm run seed` always produces the
same file). Validated by `src/lib/data/dataset.test.ts`.

## Company profile

- Danish omnichannel home & lifestyle retailer: 18 stores + webshop
- Revenue ≈ DKK 420m (FY2024) and ≈ DKK 465m (FY2025)
- Gross margin ≈ 52 %, EBITDA margin ≈ 4–8 %
- Fiscal year = calendar year; 24 months: Jan 2024 – Dec 2025; actual + budget for each month

| Category | Budget ASP 2024 (stores) | Planned gross margin | Seasonality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home & Living | 420 kr. | 52 % | Strong Nov–Dec (Christmas) |
| Fashion | 310 kr. | 58 % | Spring and Q4 peaks |
| Outdoor & Sport | 560 kr. | 46 % | Summer peak (May–Jul) |

Channels: Stores and E-commerce (28 % of units online in budget 2024, 31 % in 2025;
online prices 3 % below stores). January sale (−12 % ASP, −4 pp margin) and Black Friday in
November (−6 % ASP, −2 pp margin) are in both budget and actual.

Departments carry opex: Stores, E-commerce, Marketing, Logistics, Head office.
Rent is included in operating expenses (a pre-IFRS 16 management view; under IFRS 16 most
rent would sit below EBITDA as depreciation and interest).

## Storyline: planted budget deviations

These are the *hidden truths* the data was built from. The app does **not** know or state
these causes; it should only surface the resulting variances. They let us check that the
analysis finds what it should.

| ID | Period | What happened (fictional) | Effect in data |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Mar–May 2025 | Fashion collection sold poorly | Fashion units −14 %, ASP −5 %, margin −3,5 pp |
| B | 2025 | Webshop outperformed | E-commerce units +4,7 % (Jan) rising to +12,4 % (Dec) |
| C | Jun–Jul 2024 / May–Jul 2025 | Weather-sensitive outdoor demand | Outdoor units −12 % (2024) / +7 % (2025) |
| D | Nov–Dec 2025 | Strong Christmas season | Home & Living units +8 % |
| E | Jul–Dec 2024 | Higher purchase prices | Home & Living margin −1,5 pp |
| F | Jul–Dec 2024 | Freight rates rose | Logistics & freight +18 % |
| G | Nov 2025 | Black Friday campaign overspend | Marketing +35 % |
| H | Dec 2024 | Store overtime | Store personnel +6 % |
| I | 2025 | New IT platform | Head-office IT +10 % |
| J | 2024–2025 | Cost discipline | Other opex −5 % |

On top of this, actuals carry small random noise (±3 % units, ±1 % price, ±0,5 pp margin,
±2 % opex) so the data does not look artificially clean. D&A has no noise.

**Net result:** FY2024 EBITDA misses budget (≈ 17m vs 21m); FY2025 beats it (≈ 36m vs 32m).

## Simplifications

No VAT, inventory, returns, balance sheet or cash flow. COGS is derived from gross margin
assumptions rather than from purchase-price × units.
